Important Fare & Reservation Guidelines:

Unreserved 2nd & 3rd class tickets cannot be purchased in advance and must be bought on the day of travel at Ella Station ticket counters (usually opens 45-60 minutes before departure). Reserved 1st Class AC, Observation Saloon, and reserved 2nd/3rd class seats open 30 days prior through official SLR mobile apps and counters. Special tourist services such as the 'Ella Odyssey' carry fixed premium tourist tariffs.

Ella Railway Station Information

Historical colonial station nestled amidst misty tea gardens (Elevation: 1,041 meters).

Station Specifications

  • Station Code: ELA
  • Railway Line: Main Line (Badulla branch)
  • Elevation: 1,041 m (3,415 ft)
  • Dist. from Colombo: 271.03 km (168.41 mi)
  • Platforms: 1 Main Platform (Loop siding)
  • Ticket Counter: 05:30 AM – 19:00 PM

Station Facilities

Luggage Cloakroom Available at main parcel counter (nominal fee).
Waiting Room Separate ladies & gentlemen resting areas.
Tuk-Tuk Stand Directly outside front exit gate (agreed rates).
Refreshment Stall Bottled water, Ceylon tea, and fresh snacks.

Station Location

Station Road, Ella 90090, Uva Province, Sri Lanka. Located just 400 meters from Ella town center.

About Ella Train Times

Ella Railway Station (station code: ELA) is among Sri Lanka's most renowned hill country destinations, sitting at 1,041 meters elevation along the 19th-century railway mainline built to transport Ceylon tea.

Purpose of this portal: International backpackers, hikers, and local passengers often experience difficulty locating updated schedules, fare classes, or trailheads from the station. This independent website provides verified departure tables, fare guides, and emergency numbers in English, Sinhala, and Tamil.
